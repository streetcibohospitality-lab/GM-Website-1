# Hall of Fame — Square Retro Frame Update

The Hall of Fame has been redesigned so all four food photographs use large 1:1 square image windows.

Changes:
- equal 2×2 square-photo layout on desktop
- full square food windows with item-specific crop positioning
- cream diner frame with dark outline, checker rail, corner rivets and red offset shadow
- prices moved out of the photography into the caption plate
- descriptions moved below the image so food is never covered by copy
- the former full-image blind is now a small FAN FAVOURITE badge in the frame rail
- mobile keeps a horizontal snap gallery, but every photograph remains square
- existing reveal/hover behavior is preserved and kept subtle

No menu content, prices or Hall of Fame item selection was changed.
