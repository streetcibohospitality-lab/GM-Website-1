# Reviews + Order Layout Update

## Goal
The homepage no longer spends a full screen on a single review and then another oversized screen on online ordering.

## Desktop
Section 08 (Word On The Street) and the Online Order counter are now presented together in one compact shared scene:

- Review noticeboard on the left
- Online ordering counter on the right
- Review carousel and arrows remain functional
- Swiggy, Zomato and bell interactions remain functional
- Review typography is reduced while keeping the pinned diner-note character
- Online order typography and controls are reduced to fit the shared layout

The combined scene is designed to fit comfortably within one normal desktop viewport rather than two separate oversized sections.

## Tablet / mobile
The two panels stack instead of being squeezed side-by-side. Their spacing and type remain reduced so neither becomes a full-screen takeover.

## Preserved
- All 5 review quotes
- Review navigation and counter
- District/Zomato/Yappe links
- Swiggy and Zomato order links
- Order bell interaction
- Existing franchise section directly after the combined scene
- Previous random `What Should I Order?` update
