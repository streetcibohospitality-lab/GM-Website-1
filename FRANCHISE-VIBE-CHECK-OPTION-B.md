# Franchise Vibe Check — Option B

Implemented below the existing franchise hero. The franchise hero itself is unchanged.

## Added
- Dedicated `VIBE CHECK / 24 SEC` section immediately below the hero.
- Headline: `SEE THE MONKEY BUSINESS IN MOTION.`
- Uploaded portrait reel integrated into a wide chrome diner media frame.
- Desktop preserves the full portrait reel and uses a restrained blurred poster matte in the side space rather than cropping the video.
- Mobile switches to the reel's native portrait ratio.
- Muted autoplay, loop, playsinline.
- Play/Pause, Sound On/Off and Restart controls.
- Subtle REC indicator.
- Chrome reflection sweep.
- Reduced-motion behavior pauses autoplay and removes decorative sweep.
- Video pauses when the browser tab is hidden and resumes only when appropriate.

## Media optimization
The original upload was about 14.6 MB. The web-ready H.264/AAC version is about 4.6 MB with fast-start enabled.
