04The Story

Born in Manipal.
Raised Across Karnataka.

What started as a student hangout in Manipal grew into a 1960s-inspired American diner built around checkerboard floors, red booths, vintage wall art and unapologetically heavy grub.

From late-night cravings to weekend tables, Grub Monkeys is about food with attitude and a diner experience people remember.
