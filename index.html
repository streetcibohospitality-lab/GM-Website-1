Our Story

Born in Manipal.
Raised in Bengaluru.

What started as the most popular student hangout in Manipal grew into Bengaluru's own retro American diner — checkerboard floors, red booths, vintage wall art, and a board game on every table.

The food is unapologetically heavy, indulgent, and built for the brave. From late-night student cravings to family weekends, GRUB MONKEYS feeds the hunger that won't settle for ordinary.
