04The Story

Born in Manipal.
Raised Across Karnataka.

It started in Manipal: American-diner grub, generous portions and a room built for staying longer than planned. Today, Grub Monkeys runs across Karnataka — but Manipal is still where the story starts.

The formula stayed simple: burgers, wings, fries, thick shakes, loud music and a diner experience with enough character to remember.
