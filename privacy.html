SITE / PRIVACY

PRIVACY
POLICY.

A short explanation of the information submitted through the Grub Monkeys website and how it is used.

01INFORMATION YOU SUBMIT

Information you submit

The franchise enquiry form asks for information such as your name, phone number, email address, city of interest, investment budget and any message you choose to provide.

02HOW WE USE IT

How we use it

Information submitted through the franchise form is used to review and respond to your enquiry, discuss franchise opportunities and communicate about relevant next steps.

03FORM DELIVERY

Form delivery

The franchise enquiry form is currently transmitted using Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service. Information you submit may therefore be processed by that service in order to deliver the enquiry to the Grub Monkeys franchise team.

04YOUR CHOICE

Your choice

You do not need to submit the franchise form to browse the website. Only provide information you are comfortable sharing for the purpose of a franchise enquiry.

05PRIVACY QUESTIONS

Privacy questions

For privacy questions relating to information submitted through the website, contact Streetcibo Hospitality Pvt Ltd at streetcibohospitality@gmail.com.
